# Domain Inventory

## Purpose
Catalog of domain entities, boundaries, and high-level relationships across the platform.

## Core Entities
1. **Users & Identities**: Customers, property seekers, identity credentials, contact verifications.
2. **Brokers & Agencies**: Broker profiles, license verifications, agency associations, commissions.
3. **Properties & Listings**: Commercial and residential properties, units, pricing, media, specifications.
4. **Engagements & Inquiries**: Direct inquiries, visit schedules, chat/leads, feedback.
5. **Transactions & Assets**: Asset agreements, payment milestones, tokenized/fractional stakes.
